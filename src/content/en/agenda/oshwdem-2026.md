### OSHWDem 2026  

On October 3, 2026, we will travel to [A Coruña](https://www.google.es/maps/place/Museo+Domus/@43.3777613,-8.4074032,18z/data=!4m6!3m5!1s0xd2e7c7043c99b31:0x5c3b6eb8306f7019!8m2!3d43.3777801!4d-8.406646!16s%2Fg%2F121g0xlc?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI1MDMzMC4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D), to the Museo Domus, to participate in the [OSHWDem 2026 Competitions](https://www.oshwdem.org/), the open technologies fair featuring electronics, 3D printing, workshops, robot battles and competitions.  

See you there, hackers!
