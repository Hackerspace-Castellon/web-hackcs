### Microcourse AlumniSAUJI: Cybersecurity
On March 27, 2026, two of our members will be conducting a beginner's Cybersecurity microcourse.

Cybersecurity is not just a technical thing; it’s a basic practice for anyone who uses the Internet, and in this session, you’ll learn how to protect your personal information, recognize common threats, and use your devices securely in a practical way.

The goal is clear: that by the end of the course, you can navigate with confidence and critical thinking.

For more info, visit [web de AlumniSAUJI]([https://www.uji.es/serveis/alumnisauji/base/formacio/formacio-alumni/reservori2526/microcursos/impressio-3D/](https://www.uji.es/serveis/alumnisauji/base/formacio/formacio-alumni/reservori2526/microcursos/ciberseguretat/))
