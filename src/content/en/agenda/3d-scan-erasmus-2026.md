### Mobile 3D Scanning Workshop for Erasmus Students 2026
On February 20, 2026, a 3D scanning workshop will be held for international students.

We invite you to participate in our 3D Scanning Workshop on Friday, February 20th at 17:30.

Learn to scan in 3D with Hackerspace Castellón! Using only your smartphone, discover the magic of photogrammetry in this practical workshop. Create incredible 3D models of yourself and your friends that you can share on social media or even use for 3D printing.

No prior experience is required: join us for an afternoon of technological fun!

Meeting point: [Student House](https://maps.app.goo.gl/fhRmSUaAwBh8u4GA9)

See you!
