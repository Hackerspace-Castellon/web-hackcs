### Insomni’Hack 2025
On March 14th and 15th, 2025, we will attend [Insomni’Hack
2025](https://insomnihack.ch/), a prestigious international Capture
The Flag (CTF) competition held in Lausanne, Switzerland.
The event will gather participants from around the world to test
their skills in cybersecurity and ethical hacking.
Our team will represent Universitat Jaume I at this international
gathering, sharing experiences and knowledge with other experts in
the field.
