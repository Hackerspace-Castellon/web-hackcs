### GIS Technologies Presentation
On June 16th, 2025, we will attend a presentation on GIS
technologies and indoor geolocation using Bluetooth, delivered by
Teodor Din, researcher at INIT and GEOTEC at [Universitat Jaume
I](https://www.uji.es/).
This session will introduce the potential of BLE and Beacon
technologies applied to geographic information systems.
