### Robolid 2026

From April 9 to 11, 2026, we will travel to Valladolid to participate in the [Robolid 2026](https://amuva.eii.uva.es/) competition. We will compete in the battlebots category with our combat robots RSN-25 V2 and McQueen.

See you there, hackers!
