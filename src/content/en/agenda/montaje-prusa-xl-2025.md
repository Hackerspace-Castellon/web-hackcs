### Prusa XL Assembly
On August 26th, 2025, we will assemble and set up the new **Prusa
XL** 3D printer in our lab.
This addition will significantly enhance our production and
prototyping capacity, allowing us to streamline workflows and
develop projects with greater precision and efficiency.
