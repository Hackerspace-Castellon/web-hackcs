### SUMMER TECH 2025

On Tuesday, July 29, 2025, SUMMERTECH 2025 will be held in "El Pinar".

> ATTENTION!
> The event location has changed due to weather conditions, and all guests have been contacted. If you have not received a message, please contact the organization.

#### Information and Registration

Date: **Tuesday, July 29/07/2025**

Place: [El Pinar](https://www.google.com/maps?client=ubuntu&hs=G6J&sca_esv=eddefdb7b92e7545&channel=fs&kgmid=/g/11bxg2l97q&shndl=30&shem=lcuae,uaasie&kgs=a881b2cbc5c48ba7&um=1&ie=UTF-8&fb=1&gl=pt&sa=X&geocode=KUmmJxf1_58SMYXc8woisREW&daddr=Av.+de+Ferrandis+Salvador,+10,+12100+Grau+de+Castell%C3%B3,+Castell%C3%B3,+Espa%C3%B1a)  

  

Attend Summer Tech, an event with workshops and talks about technology and the open source world. Enjoy a unique natural environment and food while you learn and connect with experts and enthusiasts. Reserve your spot and experience this unique event.

The event is sponsored by [BLUETTI](https://www.google.com/url?sa=t&source=web&rct=j&opi=89978449&url=https://es.bluettipower.eu/%3Fsrsltid%3DAfmBOoql5f6pGZSlDPstaFGDkUotrQV6M5-Oui8Tt_y8ukrrPLGzkjIl&ved=2ahUKEwjH4cjDh-WOAxVWK_sDHfpPJyIQFnoECA0QAQ&usg=AOvVaw1e-4iRJGFVuTiH2HTzULr0), thanks to their solar panels and portable charging stations, we’ll be able to enjoy *off-grid* power throughout the event. The event is a demonstration of how it’s possible to enjoy and care for the environment at the same time.

**IMPORTANT: You must bring your own chair.**
