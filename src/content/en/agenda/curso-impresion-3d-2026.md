### Microcourse AlumniSAUJI: 3D Printing
On February 11, 16, and 18, 2026, one of our members will be offering a microcourse on 3D Printing with Prusa Slicer and parametric modeling with Onshape.

This course is designed to give you the tools you need to master the basics of 3D printing. From theoretical knowledge about the available technologies and materials, to practical skills for preparing models with Prusa Slicer and designing custom parts with Onshape.

Whether you are a beginner or already have some experience, you will find a structured way to delve into the world of additive manufacturing, with the intention of leaving with confidence and the skills needed to bring your ideas to life.

For more info, visit [web de AlumniSAUJI](https://www.uji.es/serveis/alumnisauji/base/formacio/formacio-alumni/reservori2526/microcursos/impressio-3D/)
