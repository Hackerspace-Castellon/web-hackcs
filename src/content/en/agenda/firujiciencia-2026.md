### Firujiciència 2026
On March 26, 2026, we will participate in [Firujiciència
2026](https://www.uji.es/investigacio/base/cultura-cientifica/pc4/acc-divulga/firuji/) a science divulgation fair organized at the Universitat Jaume I with the aim of bringing science closer to students and the general public.

The event will bring together numerous workshops and interactive activities dedicated to awakening scientific curiosity among the youngest.
