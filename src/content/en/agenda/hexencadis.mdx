### Introduction

Project organized by HackerSpace Castellón and La Plantilla Disseny, in collaboration with the Escuela Superior de Tecnología y Ciencias Experimentales (ESTCE).

The purpose of this project is to create a collective modular installation representative of the university community, through the design and 3D-printed manufacturing of hexagonal tiles.

The goals are:

- To promote design together with the technical innovation of 3D printing.
- To encourage originality in part modeling.
- To raise awareness of ethical responsibility in design.

This project is based on the [100 Hex Project](https://tecnoloxia.org/100hex/o-proxecto-100hex/).

### Tile

The tile will have standard measurements so the mural can be assembled correctly:

The tile is a regular hexagon with 60mm sides and a thickness of 1mm. It has a 1.6mm-thick frame and a height of 2mm.

![](images/hexencadis-plano-tesela.png)

- Modifications must be made within the 2mm space left by the frame.
- The hexagon can never be pierced through, since the back face will be used to glue it to the mural.
- The hexagon cannot have a thickness greater than 3mm.
- The hexagon will be manufactured using an FDM (3D printing) machine. The first millimeter will be printed in one of four colors (red, yellow, green and blue), while the last two millimeters will be printed in white.
- The hexagon must be able to be manufactured as a single piece.

### Rules

#### 1. Participants

Students enrolled at ESTCE may participate, in accordance with the conditions established by the associations.

#### 3. Tile specifications

The tile must strictly comply with the following specifications:

- Match the shape and dimensions shown in the sketch and template provided by the associations.
- Have no modification to the outer perimeter, to ensure the correct modular fit with the rest of the tiles.
- Be a completely original design.

#### 4. Content and ethical criteria

It is strictly forbidden to include in the design:

- Offensive, violent, discriminatory or explicit content.
- References to drugs or illegal activities.
- Partisan political content.
- Hate speech or sensitive symbols.
- QR codes.
- Unauthorized use of protected intellectual property.

The organization reserves the right to exclude any design it considers inappropriate or contrary to academic and community values.

### Dates and registration

The deadline to participate is **January 31, 2027**.

To participate you only need to submit your design through a form: the organization handles the 3D printing of the piece, participants only take care of the design.

### Contest

To encourage participation in the project, a contest will be held to award the best tile among those submitted. The tiles will be judged by a jury made up of members of both associations. Association members may enter their own tiles for the mural but cannot compete for the prize.

The prize will be a Bambu Lab A1 Mini.
