### AutoCAD Workshop
On October 22nd, 2024, one of our members will deliver a practical
AutoCAD workshop focused on 3D design, introducing students to the
basic tools for three-dimensional modeling. During the session,
participants will learn about the main functions for creating and
modifying volumes, extrusions, and surfaces, as well as techniques
for preparing models for digital fabrication and 3D printing.
The activity will help participants become familiar with the 3D
workflow within AutoCAD and understand how this tool can be used in
prototyping and technical design processes.
