### Industrial Design Engineering Seminar
On April 15th, 2025, we will take part in a seminar of the
Industrial Design Engineering and Product Development degree at
[Universitat Jaume I](https://www.uji.es/), sharing a session
focused on prototyping and digital fabrication.
The activity will bring students closer to the maker world and to
the potential of additive manufacturing technologies applied to
design.
